StackTale Publisher Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

StackTale Publisher is a private tool used only by the StackTale channel owner to manage the StackTale YouTube channel. It is not available to other users.

What data it accesses

Using YouTube API Services, it accesses only the StackTale channel's own data: uploading and scheduling videos, setting thumbnails and captions, posting and reading comments, and reading channel and video analytics. It does not access any other Google data (such as Gmail, Drive or contacts).

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to publish StackTale videos and decide what content to make next. The access token is stored privately on the owner's own machine. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions.

StackTale Publisher uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/StackTale-AI/stacktale-ai.github.io.
